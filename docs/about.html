Digitalmod Academy is a skills-focused educational institution based at Sungawila Junction, Polonnaruwa, Sri Lanka.

Our aim is to help students, school leavers, job seekers and aspiring professionals build digital literacy, creativity and professional confidence through structured, practical learning.

The proposed online catalogue brings together languages, psychology, HR and administration, technology, design and business skills.
