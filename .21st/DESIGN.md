# Digitalmod Academy design context

The deployed site is out/: plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Yellow/gold accents, cream Light mode, charcoal Dark mode, translucent glass panels and system fonts. No framework or animation dependency.

The 30-course catalogue is proposed. Course details morph from cards. Enrolment is reviewed locally and shared explicitly through WhatsApp.

Motion: session intro, replayable IntersectionObserver effects, native View Transitions with fade fallbacks, small parallax and pointer tilt. OS reduced motion and the visitor toggle disable decorative movement.

Home uses a full-screen cinematic film stage, floating dark glass navigation and gold italic serif emphasis. Its 185svh scroll runway pins a 100svh stage; native passive scroll events seek the film in both directions and gently inset the frame. Reduced motion / Motion off collapses the runway into a static first screen. The native video and poster work without a framework.
